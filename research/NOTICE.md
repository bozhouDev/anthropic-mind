and video transcripts, and personal essays and X content by Anthropic staff.

claim in this repository cites verbatim English source text and a file path, and
the corpus is retained so those claims can be independently traced, checked, and

Collection window: 2025-01-01 to 2026-08-16, plus several standing canonical
exceptions. RSP, System Cards, risk reports, Usage Policy, and Newsroom release
