## 2025-07-07 17:39:06 · jackclarkSF · 1942277208611119317

The main ideas are that AI developers should publish a "Secure Development Framework" explaining how they assess risks from CBRN threats and autonomous AI behavior, and make this framework public.

## 2025-07-07 17:39:06 · jackclarkSF · 1942277206820151701

The transparency framework focuses on the largest frontier model developers only and includes ideas for ways to exempt startups and smaller developers to avoid burdening the ecosystem while focusing on the highest-risk systems.

## 2025-07-07 17:39:06 · jackclarkSF · 1942277205209555184

For the last few months I’ve brought up ‘transparency’ as a policy framework for governing powerful AI systems and the companies that develop them - to help move this conversation forward @anthropicai has published details about what a transparency framework could look like

## 2025-07-07 17:39:07 · jackclarkSF · 1942277211735875916

We’ve written this framework in such a way that it could be applied at federal, state, or international levels, and are sharing it to try and contribute more specific ideas to the public conversation around building a thriving AI ecosystem. Details here: https://www.anthropic.com/news/the-need-for-transparency-in-frontier-ai

## 2025-07-07 17:39:07 · jackclarkSF · 1942277210188226732

We also think companies should publish “system cards” when they release models, &amp; these cards should document the testing, evaluation, and mitigations they’ve applied to their models. Think of this as equivalent to the ‘ingredients’ labels you find on things in the supermarket.
