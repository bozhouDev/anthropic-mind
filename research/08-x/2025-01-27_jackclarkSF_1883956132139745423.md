## 2025-01-27 19:11:58 · jackclarkSF · 1883956132139745423

The most surprising part of DeepSeek-R1 is that it only takes ~800k samples of 'good' RL reasoning to convert other models into RL-reasoners. Now that DeepSeek-R1 is available people will be able to refine samples out of it to convert any other model into an RL reasoner. https://x.com/jackclarkSF/status/1883956132139745423

## 2025-01-27 19:12:45 · jackclarkSF · 1883956330127581669

This is a classic one-way ratchet - the R1 release means that _all_ AI models worldwide can now be modified to improve in capabilities (with the goodness of the improvement scaling with the size/quality of the model, so bigger models will do better).

## 2025-01-27 19:24:21 · jackclarkSF · 1883959245902880892

R1 will itself be useful for generating some of the samples for the larger training dataset, as it produces high quality CoT. In addition, the RL tricks will allow for refinement of other parts of this dataset from base models (as people are already doing).
