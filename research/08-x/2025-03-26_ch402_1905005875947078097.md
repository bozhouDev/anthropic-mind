## 2025-03-26 21:16:09 · ch402 · 1905005878111358981

One thing that's kind of exciting is that I think we're moving towards having a range of agendas *within the same paradigm*.

The classic in pre-paradigmatic science is to have different schools with "Incommensurable" frameworks. Debates between them involve long philosophical treatises debating basic abstractions and ideas. (See below discussion from https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/incommensurability/ )

I think we're now seeing a much more normal scientific discourse, where we roughly agree on abstractions and basic framing, and can explore what approaches are best.
