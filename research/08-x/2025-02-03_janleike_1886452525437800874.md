## 2025-02-03 16:31:45 · janleike · 1886452525437800874

Super exciting robustness result:

We built a system that defends against universal jailbreaks!

It has minimal increase in refusal rate and moderate inference cost.

## 2025-02-03 16:31:45 · janleike · 1886452527153164762

Why do we do this?

LLMs are trained to refuse harmful tasks e.g. related to making chemical weapons.

But this safety training can be overridden with jailbreaks. Right now every LLM is vulnerable.

## 2025-02-03 16:31:46 · janleike · 1886452528990302645

We add two classifiers on top of an LLM to block inputs and outputs.

Jailbreaking our system requires jailbreaking all three models at the same time: the LLM and our two classifiers.

## 2025-02-03 16:31:46 · janleike · 1886452530890338655

Our goal is to defend against universal jailbreaks that unlock the model on many queries at once, because they enable repeated misuse.

We believe that finding new jailbreaks for every query is annoying enough that most people will give up.

## 2025-02-03 16:31:47 · janleike · 1886452532664516851

Experts tried breaking an earlier prototype for >3,000 hours and no one found a universal jailbreak.

But our system isn't perfect, and it's still possible to jailbreak the model on single queries.

## 2025-02-03 16:31:47 · janleike · 1886452534807826765

But robustness to jailbreaks is easy: just refuse all tasks.

The hard part is at the same time minimizing over-refusals and inference costs. This is what makes our system commercially viable.
