The final report from Gov Newsom’s working group on AI frontier models is out and it touches on things we @AnthropicAI care about, specifically transparency into AI development and potential risks from increasingly powerful systems. https://www.cafrontieraigov.org/

## 2025-06-18 23:51:53 · jackclarkSF · 1935485652860272789

Also of note is the idea that we should explore transparency for effects of AI on the economy writ large. Specifically, “how foundation models are deployed across the economy”.This was our idea with the Economic Index and we have more planned here.

## 2025-06-18 23:51:53 · jackclarkSF · 1935485651375505642

There’s also an appreciation for urgency - the report notes that there’s more evidence for how AI systems could magnify CBRN risks + exhibit loss of control problems. I think we often lose sight of the ‘why’ for AI policy - potential for serious misuse and accidents is the why.

## 2025-06-18 23:51:53 · jackclarkSF · 1935485650041766243

On transparency, there’s a lot here (130 mentions!), but the key idea is that transparency approaches should be “purposeful, actionable, and outcome-focused” as well as “minimize duplicative compliance burdens.” In other words, transparency needs to be in service of something.
