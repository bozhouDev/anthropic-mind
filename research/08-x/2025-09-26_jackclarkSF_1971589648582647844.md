## 2025-09-26 14:56:17 · jackclarkSF · 1971589648582647844

Will powerful AI systems immediately transform the economy, or will they have effects that show up more slowly? This is a key question for policymakers. Here's a conversation with people from American Compass and the Burning Glass Institute where I lay out my views.

## 2025-09-26 14:56:18 · jackclarkSF · 1971589652583977000

Like many in AI, I think powerful AI will grow the economy. But I suspect GDP growth on order of 6% (this would be amazing!) while others in the AI community skew more like 20%+. I think uptake is going to be extreme in certain pockets of the economy and slow in others

## 2025-09-26 14:56:19 · jackclarkSF · 1971589657701097582

With regard to policy interventions, I continue to favor telemetry (that's why we do the @AnthropicAI economic index) - if policymakers know which jobs are impacted and where, that gives them the best data from which to respond. https://x.com/jackclarkSF/status/1971589657701097582

## 2025-09-26 14:56:20 · jackclarkSF · 1971589663476600835
